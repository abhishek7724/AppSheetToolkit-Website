Simple workflow

From AppSheet editor to export in four steps.

1

Open your app

Open the AppSheet application whose metadata you want to export, then open the toolkit side panel.

2

Choose what to export

Pick individual tables, or check All Slices and All Views. You do not need to open those screens yourself.

3

Choose a format

Select JSON, CSV, or both, then start extraction and follow the live progress.

4

Use the output

Open the files in Excel, a text editor, your documentation, or an AI workflow.

FAQ

Questions before installing?

The essentials, without the fine print.

Does AppSheet Toolkit collect my data?

No. The extension does not use analytics, tracking, or an external data-storage server.

Can I export slices and views too?

Yes. Check All Slices or All Views alongside your table selection, and everything is exported in the same run.

Do I need to open the Views screen first?

No. The toolkit switches the editor to the screens it needs and puts it back when it is finished.

Can I export JSON and CSV together?

Yes. Choose Both before starting extraction.

What happens if I cancel halfway?

Nothing is written. Files are only created once a full run completes, so a cancelled run never leaves a partial export behind.

Is this an official Google product?

No. AppSheet Toolkit is an independent third-party extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
