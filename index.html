Simple workflow

From AppSheet editor to export in four steps.

1

Open Data → Columns

Open the AppSheet application whose metadata you want to export.

2

Select tables

Choose all tables or only the tables needed for your current task.

3

Choose a format

Select JSON, CSV, or both and start extraction.

4

Use the exported files

Open the results in Excel, a text editor, an AI assistant, or your documentation workflow.
