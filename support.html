Help and support

How can we help?

Find common troubleshooting steps or send a support request, bug report, feature idea, or general feedback.

↻

Extension not detecting AppSheet

Confirm that you are on an AppSheet editor page. After installing or reloading the extension, refresh the AppSheet tab before opening the toolkit.

↓

Export did not download

Check the browser's download permissions and confirm that at least one table and output format are selected.

◔

Progress stopped

Keep the AppSheet editor tab open during extraction and avoid manually changing tables while the extension is running.

✉

Report a problem

Include your browser version, the AppSheet screen being used, and a screenshot of any visible error.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does AppSheet Toolkit collect my data?

No. The extension does not use analytics, tracking, or an external data-storage server.

Where are exported files saved?

Files are saved according to your browser's normal download settings.

Can I export only selected tables?

Yes. Select individual tables or use the All Tables option.

Can I export JSON and CSV together?

Yes. Choose Both before starting extraction.

Is this an official Google product?

No. AppSheet Toolkit is an independent third-party extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
