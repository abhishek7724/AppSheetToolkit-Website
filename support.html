Help and support

How can we help?

Find common troubleshooting steps or report a problem with AppSheet Toolkit.

↻

Extension not detecting AppSheet

Confirm that you are on an AppSheet editor page. After installing or reloading the extension, refresh the AppSheet browser tab before opening the toolkit.

⬇

Export did not download

Check the browser's download permissions and confirm that at least one table and one output format are selected.

◔

Progress stopped

Keep the AppSheet editor tab open during extraction. Avoid manually changing tables while the extension is running.

✉

Report an issue

Include the browser version, AppSheet screen being used, and a screenshot of any visible error.

Contact

Need additional help?

Submit an issue through the public support repository. Do not include passwords, confidential business data, or private application metadata.

Support & Feedback
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does AppSheet Toolkit collect my data?

No. The extension does not use analytics, tracking, or an external data-storage server.

Where are exported files saved?

Files are saved according to your browser's normal download settings.

Can I export only selected tables?

Yes. Select individual tables or use the All Tables option.

Can I export JSON and CSV together?

Yes. Choose the Both option before starting extraction.

Is this an official Google product?

No. AppSheet Toolkit is an independent third-party extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
